Pillar 01

Plan and measure

Know what to do, what to say, and what is working.

  1. 01

    Digital strategy

    One plan from business goal to channel, so every dollar has a job.

    I start from the number the business needs, whether that is revenue, leads or retention, and work back to the audience, channels, budget and measures that will move it.

    What I deliver

    • Goals, KPIs and baselines leadership signs off on
    • Audience and customer-journey mapping
    • Channel mix and budget set by priority
    • A 12-month roadmap with quarterly checkpoints

    KPIs I own

    • Revenue growth
    • Customer acquisition cost
    • Marketing ROI
    • Pipeline

    Track record

    Vehicle dealership: six sales pushes, each optimized for its own result, delivered 1,779 leads and 1,217 event responses. Read the case: Vehicle Dealership

    Energy drink brand: budget organized by objective, month and audience, with territories tiered by trade volume. Read the case: Energy Drink Brand

  2. 02

    Content strategy

    The right message, in the right format, at every stage of the journey.

    I decide what to say, to whom, in which format and in what order, then test it. Content is planned against the funnel, so every piece has a job and a measure.

    What I deliver

    • Messaging and content pillars tied to business goals
    • Formats planned by journey stage, from first view to repurchase
    • Creative briefs and testing plans
    • Creator and influencer content that earns trust

    KPIs I own

    • Engagement rate
    • View-through rate
    • Cost per engagement
    • Content-assisted conversions

    Track record

    Energy drink brand: a five-stage journey, from ad recall to repurchase, with formats chosen for each stage. Read the case: Energy Drink Brand

    Energy drink launch: three versions of one ad tested on near-equal impressions; version B delivered 19% more estimated ad recall lift than version A. Read the case: Energy Drink Brand

  3. 03

    Digital analytics

    Numbers leadership can trust, and act on.

    If you can’t see it, you can’t scale it. I set up the tracking, benchmarks and dashboards that tie marketing to business results, then turn them into decisions.

    What I deliver

    • Conversion tracking and tagging audits
    • Attribution across channels, online and in store
    • Benchmarks and targets for every channel
    • Executive dashboards and plain-language reports

    KPIs I own

    • Share of sales tracked
    • Cost per result by channel
    • Time to insight
    • Forecast accuracy

    Track record

    Professional sports: a tracked 57% of ticket sales came through the online ticket page. Read the case: Professional Sports

    Insurance provider: a Meta lead cost $1.74 USD in Trinidad and Tobago and $4.20 in Barbados, which pointed each market to a different channel. Read the case: Insurance Provider

Pillar 02

Reach and engage

Put the message in front of the right people, and keep them talking.

  1. 04

    Paid media

    Ad spend that answers to a number.

    12 years and more than $10M USD managed across Meta, Google, social and programmatic. I buy for the action that matters, not the cheapest impression, and I report what didn’t work as plainly as what did.

    What I deliver

    • Full-funnel campaign plans
    • Build, launch and weekly optimization
    • Creative testing and influencer Paid Partnerships
    • Meta, Google Search, Display and YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, Spotify, Bing and programmatic

    KPIs I own

    • Cost per lead
    • Return on ad spend
    • Conversion rate
    • Cost per acquisition

    Track record

    Insurance provider, Jamaica: 614 leads against a target of 500, at $1.95 USD per lead and falling as the campaign ran. Read the case: Insurance Provider

    Energy drink contest 2.0: 485,369 participations at $0.31 USD per entry. Read the case: Energy Drink Brand

  2. 05

    Social media management

    Channels that build the brand and feed the pipeline, every week.

    I run your social channels as one system with paid media: the posts that perform organically get promoted, creators extend the reach, and every channel reports against a business goal.

    What I deliver

    • Channel strategy and content calendars
    • Publishing, replies and moderation workflows
    • Winning organic posts promoted into the media mix
    • Influencer and creator Paid Partnerships
    • Monthly reporting on reach, engagement and conversions

    KPIs I own

    • Engagement rate
    • Follower growth
    • Share of voice
    • Social-sourced leads

    Track record

    Energy drink contest 2.0: each week, the best organic posts by engagement were promoted into the media mix and tested. Read the case: Energy Drink Brand

    Confectionery brand: monthly content calendars in four markets, with 3.23M post engagements across 90 campaigns. Read the case: Confectionery Brand

  3. 06

    Community building and engagement

    An audience that talks back, shares and comes back.

    Reach is rented. Community is owned. I build the conversations, moments and rituals that turn followers into advocates, and measure engagement past the vanity numbers.

    What I deliver

    • Always-on community management and response standards
    • Engagement campaigns, contests and user-generated content
    • Advocate programs with staff, customers and creators
    • Sentiment and engagement reporting

    KPIs I own

    • Engagement rate
    • Community growth
    • Response time
    • Shares and advocacy

    Track record

    Confectionery brand: a 2024 promotion in Trinidad and Tobago drew 554,852 post engagements. Read the case: Confectionery Brand

    Spirits brand: the mystery shopper videos drew 334,791 two-second plays from 306,799 people. Read the case: Spirits Brand

Convert and scale

Turn interest into revenue, and make it repeatable.

  1. 07

    Email marketing

    Revenue from the audience you already own.

    What I deliver

    • A campaign calendar tied to sales priorities
    • Segmentation and personalization
    • Tests on subject lines, offers and timing
    • List growth from paid and organic channels

    KPIs I own

    • Revenue per subscriber
    • Click rate
    • List growth
    • Repeat purchase rate

    Track record

    Commercial bank: email ran alongside Meta, Google, Bing, Spotify and LinkedIn in the Christmas 2024 loans campaign. Read the case: Commercial Bank

  2. 08

    Marketing automation

    Every lead followed up and nurtured, without adding headcount.

    I map the journey from first click to customer, then automate the follow-up, scoring and hand-offs that slip through the cracks when people do them by hand.

    What I deliver

    • Lead capture, routing and instant follow-up
    • Nurture journeys triggered by behaviour
    • Lead scoring and sales hand-off rules
    • Connections between your CRM and ad platforms, so results flow into reporting

    KPIs I own

    • Lead-to-customer rate
    • Speed to lead
    • Cost per acquisition
    • Hours saved
  3. 09

    Training and development

    Teams that can plan, run and read their own campaigns.

    If you can’t explain it, you don’t control it. I teach the frameworks behind the results, so the capability stays in your team after the workshop ends.

    What I deliver

    • Workshops: Meta Ads 101, campaign planning, digital media analytics
    • Playbooks and templates your team keeps
    • Coaching for in-house marketers
    • Onboarding for new channels and tools

    KPIs I own

    • Time to competency
    • Work done in-house
    • Tool adoption
    • Cost of outside support

    Track record

    Meta Ads 101 for Toronto Metropolitan University’s The Creative School, and Academic Assistant for three TMU courses.

The process

Four steps from goal to report

The same sequence behind every case study on this site.

01

Goal and audit

Agree the business result, the KPI that proves it, and what is already working in your channels, email, automation and tracking.

02

Plan and build

Audience, channel mix, journeys, creative and budget, built with tracking in place before launch.

03

Launch and learn

Enough creative and enough time for the platforms to learn, with weekly checks on cost, quality and conversion.

04

Optimize and report

Move budget to what converts, automate what repeats, and report results against the KPIs, with what I would change next.

Questions

Frequently asked

Something else on your mind? Ask me directly

What does Warren Chanansingh do?

Warren Chanansingh is a Toronto digital strategist. He helps companies turn marketing spend into measurable growth through digital strategy, content strategy, digital analytics, paid media, social media management, community building and engagement, email marketing, marketing automation, and training and development. He has 12 years of experience and has managed more than $10M USD in media.

Which KPIs do you report on?

The ones leadership already tracks: revenue, customer acquisition cost, marketing ROI and pipeline. Under those sit the channel measures that explain them, such as cost per lead, return on ad spend, engagement rate, click rate, list growth and lead-to-customer rate.

Which platforms do you work in?

For paid media: Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google Search, Display and YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, Spotify, Bing and programmatic networks. The case studies on this site show each of them in use. For email, automation and analytics, I start with the platforms your team already has.

How do you report results?

Against the KPIs agreed at the start, in plain language: what moved, what didn’t, and what I would change next. The case studies on this site follow the same format.

Can you train my team?

Yes. I deliver workshops on campaign planning, Meta Ads and digital media analytics, including "Meta Ads 101" for Toronto Metropolitan University’s The Creative School.

Where are you based?

Toronto, Ontario. My case studies cover campaigns in 14 markets: 12 in the Caribbean, from Trinidad and Tobago, Jamaica, Barbados and Guyana to the Cayman Islands and the Eastern Caribbean, plus Bermuda and Florida.

Do you work in-house or as a consultant?

Both. I’m open to senior in-house digital strategy roles in Toronto, with the next start in January 2027, and I take on a small number of consulting engagements and workshops.
